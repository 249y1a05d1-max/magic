Akbar and Birbal

The Mystery of the Real Thief

👑 🏰 🔍

The Story Begins

One day, a merchant came to Emperor Akbar's court and complained that some of his money had been stolen. He suspected one of his servants.

Akbar asked Birbal to solve the mystery. Birbal called all the servants and gave each of them a stick of the same length.

Birbal told them, "The stick belonging to the thief will become two inches longer by tomorrow morning."

The servants went home with their sticks. The next morning, they returned to the court. Birbal carefully checked every stick.

One servant's stick was shorter than the others. Birbal immediately identified him as the thief.

How Did Birbal Find the Thief?

Can you guess why the thief's stick was shorter? Click the button below to discover Birbal's clever trick!

Birbal's clever idea: The frightened thief believed his stick would become longer, so he secretly cut two inches off it to avoid being caught. Birbal noticed the shorter stick and identified the thief.

Moral of the Story

Truth and clever thinking can help uncover dishonesty.
